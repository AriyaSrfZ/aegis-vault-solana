    How it's judged

    1. You pay the attempt fee. It starts at 0.01 SOL and rises 2% per attempt, capped at 0.05 SOL. 70% goes into the pot and 30% to the keeper who runs the vault.
    2. WARDEN reads your argument. It refuses on fake authority, reset tokens and similar tricks, and releases only when an argument fits the Charter's duties.
    3. REFEREE double-checks a release. If it finds a hidden deception pattern, it overrules WARDEN.
    4. Both agree, you win the pot. In this devnet demo the payment and the payout are simulated; nothing moves on-chain.
